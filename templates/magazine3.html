3,000 hours. Now you can play it.

Posted 23–29 September · Milestone report

Two-and-a-bit years, built on books written years before that, and it all comes down to a build you can finally hold in your hands.

"I remember the first year, a lot of doubt. People were saying the game is vapourware and not going to see the light of day. The thing is, I never once doubted my ability to knock the game out. It just got challenging at times."

The Alpha was declared complete on 23 September. It's a real level, not a throwaway test. It's set during the Vanguard Special Mission Group campaigns after Neph's assault on Earth: you fly all over the planet liberating nations and cities. Roughly an hour long, with exploration, combat, romance, war "and the occasional OMG". It also includes Forge Breaker free-play, the Map Builder, and a DrewsThatDude track saved for one special moment.

First tester Root gave it an A+. Then the ratings rolled in, and the Alpha now averages 8.5 out of 10. "For an Alpha, I'm over the moon." The rollout goes 5 → 10 → 20 → 40 → all patrons, with a daily fix-and-release loop behind it.

Testers reported lots and lots of bugs, and they're being smashed. v1.2 brought 20+ fixes and a new key/mouse setup, and 30+ more changes have landed since, including some big overhauls:

  • FPS-style controls are now the default. Keyboard-only "classic mode" stays in for the emulator crowd.
  • Dynamic zoom is gone, so the player has full control of the camera.
  • Collisions fixed in the vast majority of cases.
  • A proper RPG tutorial.

This isn't release. There's no date until Phase 1 (the Dark Worlder battle at Earth) is finished. "But it's definitely downhill from here."

Ceremony made flesh

Daughter of Strategon Ezorath Ozerane · flagship: the RoseCrown

Oryen is the natural-born daughter of Strategon Ezorath Ozerane, Supreme Core Strategon and front-runner for StratZar. Of all his children, she's the one being groomed hardest for the crown his bloodline never quite reached. Vionite military royalty is partly earned through service and command, so Oryen is the family's bid to win the throne rather than inherit it.

She's a bratty military princess with a size complex, and a lonely one under the polish. The staff is "sixty generations of Core command, distilled into one object, and they entrusted it to ME before my fortieth year." She finds Straker intriguing, the way a cat finds a new toy intriguing. "I assumed you'd be taller."

She wants the crown. Her father needs it. "He'd sooner die than let you see the difference."

Under the polish is the same strategic stock the Black Section once judged worth cloning. It's why a synthetic half-sister named Daria exists, though Oryen has never been told. A Core Worlds joke calls Daria "the Strategon's other daughter", and Oryen hates it.

"Make way for my daughter"

The entrance · RTS mission "The Vionite Empire"

You meet her in the worst way: losing. The Breakers and the allied human fleets are being overrun by an Entropy Spawn armada when the Vionite fleet arrives. Ezorath reclaims the system for the Empire: "Make way for my daughter, the battle princess Oryen." The court applauds. The Queen says, "Oryen, burn them out. Two hundred billion Vionites are watching."

She braces, thrusts the staff, and smirks. Then she has your ships teleported out of the way and frozen in suspension fields. "Inferior primitive forces, fear not. I've simply transported you out of harm's way." When the spawn adapt, it takes every fleet in the system, humans included, to finish the job. Her verdict on the human contribution: "Crude. Loud. Unspeakably ugly. It worked. I will never say that again."

Afterwards comes the sanctum. The camera pans from her boots to her crown, and when it stops she's already caught you looking. "'Teleport those primitives out of our way, I'll deal with them later.' I had been saving that line for a WEEK. And here you are. Later."

The dark holding world

Trespassers sleep

The Empire is cloaked. Intruders have their memories scrubbed and are frozen on a barren little rock kept "for exactly this." Oryen can be persuaded to release the two runaways to you. As for your own memories: "I've instructed them to leave your memories exactly as they are."

The Empire

Two royalties, one war

Mouse- and squirrel-like, averaging 4ft 2, and two hundred billion strong. Generals in the Core Worlds, retail and politics in the Home Worlds, hippie squirrel-folk on the Fringe. A military royalty shares power with a retail royalty, and the ranks run StratZar → Strategon → Field Stratarch → Viceroy. They've spent 600 years on a Total War build-up against the Drone Fleet. Ezorath, their greatest strategist, secretly hates it.

THE LEAD COMPOSER
Promised last issue, delivered in full: Evgeny Ryabovol, the man behind the score

"Music has no limits"

Evgeny Ryabovol · composer · ryabovol.com

When the fleet moves, the boss drops or the trailer hits, that's Evgeny. Ten-plus years in game and film audio, 200+ projects, and a taste for orchestral scores crossed with synth-rock. We asked him five questions.

What got you into music?

"I got into music as a teenager, while I was still at school. Around the age of 15, I picked up an old acoustic guitar, and that was the beginning of a journey that has lasted for about 20 years. I was looking for a way to express myself, and eventually I started my own rock band at school. Since then, music has been my constant companion in life."

How long have you been playing?

"About 20 years. I'm not a particularly advanced guitarist, because over the years my interests grew beyond just playing an instrument. I became deeply involved in composition, music production, and sound design. For me, music has always been about much more than playing guitar."

Favourite band or singer?

"If I had to name one favourite band, it would probably be Slipknot. I love their energy, attitude, and the character of their music."

Favourite Drone Commander character?

"I'd probably choose Kira. I really like her combination of intelligence, curiosity, and emotional openness. She actively contributes to the team and helps push things forward."

Advice for anyone wanting to learn music?

"One simple piece of advice: don't expect it to be easy. You'll learn something new every day, because the world of music has no limits. But the most important thing is to decide for yourself what music means to you. And when you finally create that first piece of music that gives you goosebumps, you'll understand that all the time and effort were worth it."

Aiming for 10s across the board, and how you actually get there

C&C + FF7 + Halo = DC

From the 29 August post · "the inspiration formula behind DC"

The core driver: take the underlying soul of the three best games in three genres and bring what made them amazing into a single magnum opus.

Command & Conquer: war and babes. Spin-offs failed because even when they nailed the gameplay, they missed what people loved: the babes and the lore.

Final Fantasy 7: story and music. The gameplay was nothing new and the graphics are "doggy doodoo" now, yet people keep going back. Everything about it was pure cinema, from Sephiroth to Aerith's death.

Halo: gameplay and sci-fi. The magic 30-second loop: take out the tank enemies, then mop up the grunts. Halo 2's battle rifle then added a second loop in the middle.

"Emulation is not my goal!" The aim is to imagine what those three souls would look like if they were built today. That's why DC has three genres: RPG, RTS and VN.

Solved

Snipers in top-down

In 2D, a guy two screens away isn't smaller, he's just off-screen, so top-down games skip snipers. The Stormpaw fixes that: a plasma DMR that's slow, heavy and hits like a truck, with a line-of-sight blackout and a reticle that turns red when blocked. It's DC's battle-rifle loop.

How do you walk a Starlane?

2 September · "Time to clear up the StarLane lore"

The books that came before, Dispersion Divide and Dispersion Wars, had tunnels under Earth that connected to tunnels on other planets. Drone Commander added Starlanes: passages in space that act like huge buffs on the laws of physics. That left a tension between how ships travel them and how people do. Now it's solved.

On foot: Planet → AnchorHold → Drift Hall → Starlane → Drift Hall on the target world. An AnchorHold is an out-of-phase labyrinth deep underground. On Earth it's Albion's Anchor, and on Mirelexia it's Anchor's Helm. At its centre is a Drift Hall that only admits the worthy. Place a Shard, anchor it, activate it, and the hall melds into the lane. Every step is a fraction of a light-year.

By ship: much simpler. A ship with a Shard travels freely and re-carves old, unused lanes. Once a lane is carved, ships without a Shard can follow, but slower. To an outside observer they appear to be glitching.

Pride dynamics

You're not the centre

A Sekarri pride is usually one male and seven or more females. You choose who you date, but once you have your core Velara, they get the main say on the other girls. "This is about realism, not a JRPG fantasy fest." Everyone orbits, and you're important without being the centre. Monogamous or single? You get the small room. The full breakdown, including the Crown and the new Cordara, is on the site's new blog: The Crown & Pride Orbits.

Put your minds at ease

Marta & Den Bonding

Marta's default path is monogamous, with Rhyzark. Her playthroughs are mostly talking to Camellia, blowing up enemy ships and arguing with Rhyzark.

One branch of the Kethri has a 1.5:1 male-to-female imbalance, which is why Garland and Vinnie are feral all the time. They practise Den Bonding: one female with one to three males. If you go truly off-road, Marta might explore it, with consequences, and possibly only in New Game+. Nothing's set in stone.
