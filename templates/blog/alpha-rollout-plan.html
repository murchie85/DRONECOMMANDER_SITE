The Alpha is ready and the first tester has played it. Here is exactly how I plan to get it to you — who gets it first, why, and what happens after. No surprises.

Why a Careful Rollout

I will be straight with you. We know Drone Commander's Patreon content gets leaked — most likely someone subs for a month, copies everything, and it ends up on a mirror site. Art is one thing. But the Alpha is the whole game in progress: the code, the art, the music. There are companies that take a build like that and try to fast-track a release before the original developer. It happens.

There is still a lot of development ahead, so I want to protect the game until it is close to release. When I asked you on Patreon, you overwhelmingly said the same thing: take the slower, safer road. Thank you for that.

Who Gets the Alpha First

Priority goes to the tiers who have put the biggest stake in the game:

  • The Drone Commander tier
  • Drone Commodore tier

Then Drone Master members who signed up before a cut-off date, so a quick sub-and-dip can't grab it.

Everyone else still gets the Alpha — just nearer to release, at a point where a leak can no longer hurt the game. And the Alpha is effectively a demo, so it will also go on Steam for everyone in a few months.

It Goes Out in Waves

Rather than send it to everyone on day one, it goes out in small waves:

  • Wave 1 — 5 testers. I fix anything critical they find.
  • Wave 2 — 10 more, once wave 1 is running without serious problems.
  • Then 15, 20 and onwards, each wave only after the last one is stable.

This keeps each build solid before more people play it, and it means a bug that breaks the game only ever hits a handful of people instead of everyone.

How You'll Receive It

  • A personal download link, sent to you directly. Links expire after a few days, so if yours runs out just ask and I'll send a fresh one.
  • Licensed to you. The game opens with your name on it. It is a thank-you, and a gentle reminder that this copy is yours alone.
  • Windows. On a Mac? Just let me know — a Mac build is easy for me to make.
  • Fully offline. Nothing in the game phones home. Once it is installed, it just plays.

What I Ask of You

  • Please don't share the build or its art. Every leak makes it harder to open the Alpha up to more people.
  • Report bugs through the Bug Reporter. Tell me what you were doing when it broke — that is gold.
  • Tell me what you loved, and what dragged. That shapes the next build.

After the Alpha

There will be at least one more demo, with different content and characters. Each round follows the same careful pattern, opening up wider as release gets closer.

Thank you for backing this game and for looking out for it. It means more than you know.
